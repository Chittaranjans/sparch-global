Build a Job Ingestion & Search Service:
– Two different sources each provide an array of raw job postings.
– You need to ingest them into a unified schema.
– Deduplicate jobs (e.g., same role posted multiple times) across sources.
– Normalize key fields: job title, location (city/country), salary range if present.
– Provide an endpoint to query jobs by keyword and location.
In ~30 minutes, aim for:
– Ingestion functions for 2 sources.
– Dedup and normalization.
– A simple search endpoint.
